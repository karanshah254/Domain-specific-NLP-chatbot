### NLP PROCESSING PIPELINE - CONVERST HUMAN TEXT -> MACHINE UNDERSTANBLE FORMAT

#### STEP 1: Text Cleaning
- >Remove unwanted characters
- >Example: lowercasing, remove punctuation and special characters

#### STEP 2: Tokenization
- >Split sentence into words.

#### STEP 3: Stopword Removal
- >Remove meaningless common words.

#### STEP 4: Lemmatization
- >Convert words to base form.

#### STEP 5: Text Vectorization
- >Computers cannot understand words.
- >Text to numerical vectors
